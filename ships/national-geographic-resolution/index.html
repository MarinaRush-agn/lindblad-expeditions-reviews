National Geographic Resolution Review 2026

Lindblad's current-generation X-Bow polar ship, National Geographic Resolution posts the smoothest ride and the least pre-departure complaint activity of the three Antarctic ships in this site's review sample. For the fleet-wide picture, see the Lindblad Expeditions review.

Lindblad's own current ship page states Resolution's capacity as 138 guests. Some third-party sell sheets and travel-agency sites still cite 126 — this site uses Lindblad's own live figure as the more current source.

History

Resolution's hull was built at the CRIST shipyard in Gdynia, Poland, and outfitted at Ulstein Verft in Norway. She was christened on fast ice in the Weddell Sea's Duse Bay on 24 November 2021, and is named for the ship in which Captain James Cook first crossed the Antarctic Circle in 1773.

Equipment

She carries the Polar Class 5 X-Bow hull design — an inverted bow shape engineered to cut through waves rather than slam over them — along with a full Zodiac and kayak fleet. The same hull design appears on sister ship National Geographic Endurance; it is not unique to any single operator, and Aurora Expeditions uses a comparable X-Bow design on its own fleet.
