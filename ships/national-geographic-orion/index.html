National Geographic Orion Review 2026

The one ship in Lindblad's fleet with a genuinely different history — built for another operator, acquired and renamed in 2013–14 — National Geographic Orion also sails the broadest range of regions of any ship in the fleet. For the fleet-wide picture, see the Lindblad Expeditions review.

History

Built in 2003 in Germany as MV Orion, the ship sailed for two other operators before Lindblad acquired and renamed her in 2013–14. She is not ice-classed under the Polar Code the way Explorer, Resolution and Endurance are, which is part of why her itinerary range sits outside the polar seasons entirely for much of the year.

Equipment

Around 14 Zodiacs, roughly 24 kayaks, scuba and snorkel gear, an ROV, a glass-bottom boat and a hydrophone. She lacks a dedicated mud room, a real limitation on polar sailings compared with Lindblad's newer purpose-built ships. Lindblad guarantees a National Geographic photographer on every Orion departure — one of only two ships in the fleet with that guarantee, alongside Explorer.
