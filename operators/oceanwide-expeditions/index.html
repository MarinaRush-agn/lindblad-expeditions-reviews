Oceanwide Expeditions Reviews 2026: Antarctica's Most Affordable Fleet, Scored

Oceanwide Expeditions ranks third in this ten-operator comparison with a total score of 78.8, driven by a strong shore-time score across its three ice-classed ships and the highest price/value score of any operator that actually lands passengers in Antarctica. It suits budget-focused travellers willing to trade some onboard polish for shore time and price; it does not suit travellers expecting all-inclusive luxury service.

How it scores

How this score is calculated

Effective capacity is averaged across the three ice-classed motor vessels only (Hondius, Ortelius, Plancius). The 33-berth Rembrandt van Rijn sailing vessel, which carries no ice class, is excluded — folding a sailing vessel into the same average as three motor expedition ships was methodologically indefensible, and had handed Oceanwide the highest shore-time score in the entire ranking (90.1, effective capacity 111), ahead of Poseidon. The fleet

Oceanwide runs the most varied fleet in this ranking. Hondius (174 berths, Polar Class 6) and Ortelius (123 berths, Ice Class 1A) both exceed the IAATO shore limit and rotate landing groups. Plancius (116 berths, Ice Class 1D) runs a short rotation close to the threshold. The 33-berth sailing vessel Rembrandt van Rijn carries no ice classification at all and, being under the 100-guest limit, needs no rotation — everyone goes ashore together on every landing.

What reviewers say about Oceanwide Expeditions

FAQ

Which Oceanwide Expeditions ship has the shortest landing wait?

The 33-berth Rembrandt van Rijn sailing vessel is under the IAATO 100-guest limit, so every landing goes ashore together with no rotation at all — the shortest possible wait of any ship in this ranking.

Are Oceanwide Expeditions Antarctica reviews consistent across ships?

Not entirely. Hondius reviews describe a more polished experience than the smaller, plainer ships; food-service complaints recur more on the larger vessels than on Plancius or the sailing vessel.

Is a parka included with Oceanwide Expeditions?

No — unlike most operators in this ranking, Oceanwide does not include a parka loan, one reason its price/value score of 90 sits above operators that do include one.
