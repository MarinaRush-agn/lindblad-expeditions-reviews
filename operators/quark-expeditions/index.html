Quark Expeditions Reviews 2026: The Helicopter-Equipped Fleet, Scored

Quark Expeditions ranks fifth in this ten-operator comparison with a total score of 72.6, carrying the widest route choice and the only helicopter-equipped ship among the mid-tier operators. It suits travellers who want helicopter access without Scenic's or Silversea's price ceiling; it does not suit travellers put off by the communication complaints noted below.

The fleet

Quark operates the largest fleet in this ranking: Ultramarine (199 passengers, Polar Class 6, two onboard helicopters), World Explorer (176), Ocean Explorer (138), and World Voyager (172). Every ship exceeds the 100-guest shore limit, so group rotation applies fleet-wide — but Ultramarine's helicopters offset some of the lost shore time by reaching sites a Zodiac cannot.

What reviewers say about Quark Expeditions

Aggregate rating: 4.3–4.5 (Cruise Critic / TripAdvisor)

Expedition team

Lecture and expedition staff on Ultramarine and World Explorer are consistently rated a highlight, described as knowledgeable in glaciology, ornithology and polar history.

Cruise Critic
Landings

Reviewers note efficient Zodiac operations even at full passenger loads, and several call out helicopter-supported landings on Ultramarine as a standout differentiator.

Cruise Critic
Communication

A recurring complaint concerns thin updates during weather delays, and at least one account describes a cabin reassignment made without prior notice — feedback Quark has publicly acknowledged.

TripAdvisor
Ship & cabins

Cabins across the fleet are described as unusually spacious for the category, with some suites offering heated bathroom floors.

FAQ

Do Quark Expeditions Antarctica reviews mention the helicopters?

Yes, repeatedly and positively — Ultramarine's two onboard helicopters are consistently cited as a standout differentiator for reaching sites Zodiacs cannot.

What do Quark Expeditions cruise reviews say about communication?

A recurring complaint involves thin updates during weather delays and at least one unannounced cabin reassignment; Quark has publicly acknowledged this feedback and said it would strengthen pre-voyage communication.

Are Quark Expeditions Svalbard reviews relevant to its Antarctica score?

No. This ranking scores Quark's Antarctic fleet and operations specifically; its Svalbard programme uses different ships and itineraries not covered by this scoring model.
