Lindblad Expeditions in Alaska: Reviews and What to Expect

Lindblad's two coastal ships work Alaska and the Pacific Northwest, and the review pattern here follows the same split found fleet-wide: staff and onboard design praised, cost-to-value and the occasional food complaint the recurring criticism. This page covers Alaska specifically; for Lindblad's overall rating see the Lindblad Expeditions review.

What reviewers say about Lindblad in Alaska

Based on this site's reading of the Cruise Critic sample for Lindblad's Alaska sailings

Staff & food

Crew and dining are both praised in this sample, a contrast with the food-for-price criticism that recurs more on the Antarctic ships.

Cruise Critic
Zodiac-friendly design

NG Venture's boarding platforms are specifically called out as an improvement over older expedition-ship designs for getting in and out of Zodiacs.

Cruise Critic
Cost relative to value

Some reviewers question the fare against a coastal itinerary that, unlike Antarctica or the Galápagos, competes directly with lower-priced small-ship Alaska operators.

Cruise Critic
Limited free time

A recurring complaint describes a tightly packed daily schedule with little unstructured time between activities.

FAQ

Which Lindblad ships sail to Alaska?

National Geographic Venture and National Geographic Quest, both 100-guest coastal ships built in 2018 and 2017 respectively, neither ice-classed.
