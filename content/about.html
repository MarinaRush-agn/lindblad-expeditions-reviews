About Expedition Review Desk

Expedition Review Desk reads Lindblad Expeditions' review record in full — every ship, every destination, every rating source this site could verify — and separately compares ten Antarctic expedition operators on a published methodology. This page covers what it does, what it doesn't, and how it's kept current.

What this site covers

The homepage reads Lindblad Expeditions across every rating platform this site could verify, every ship in its fleet, and every destination it sails, not just Antarctica. Separately, this site scores and compares ten Antarctic expedition cruise operators on a published methodology: shore time per passenger, traveller ratings, price and inclusions, and expedition depth. Each operator has its own page with fleet detail, review-pattern themes, and a factor-by-factor score breakdown. The methodology page documents the formula in full, and how we read reviews documents the verification rule behind every rating figure and quote on the site.

What this site does not do

This site does not sell tours, does not take bookings, and does not earn a commission on any operator's sales. It does not publish first-person trip reports from staff, because none of this site's editorial team has sailed with Lindblad Expeditions or any operator covered here — claiming otherwise would be a fabricated credential, and this site does not do that. Every claim about an operator's onboard experience is sourced from public review platforms and cited in the underlying research, not from firsthand travel.

How the data is kept current

The editorial team maintains the operator and ship dataset — fleet capacity, ice class, pricing, and IAATO membership dates — and re-checks it before each season, since fleets and capacities do change between seasons. Ratings are refreshed on the same schedule. Where a figure cannot be verified from a source the team has actually opened, it is left out rather than estimated; see how we read reviews and the methodology page for how that plays out in practice.
