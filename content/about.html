About Antarctic Review Hub

Antarctic Review Hub is an independent comparison of Antarctic expedition cruise operators. This page covers what it does, what it doesn't, and how it's kept current.

What this site covers

This site scores and compares ten Antarctic expedition cruise operators on a published methodology: shore time per passenger, traveller ratings, price and inclusions, and expedition depth. Each operator has its own page with fleet detail, review-pattern themes, and a factor-by-factor score breakdown. The methodology page documents the formula in full.

What this site does not do

This site does not sell tours, does not take bookings, and does not earn a commission on any operator's sales. It does not publish first-person trip reports from staff, because none of this site's editorial team has sailed with any operator covered here — claiming otherwise would be a fabricated credential, and this site does not do that. Every claim about an operator's onboard experience is sourced from public review platforms and cited in the underlying research, not from firsthand travel.

How the data is kept current

The editorial team maintains the operator dataset — fleet capacity, ice class, pricing, and IAATO membership dates — and re-checks it before each Antarctic season, since fleets and capacities do change between seasons. Ratings are refreshed on the same schedule. Where a figure cannot be verified from a source the team has actually opened, it is left out rather than estimated; see the methodology page for how that plays out in practice.
