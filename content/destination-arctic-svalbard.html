A widely-read Svalbard review, examined

A review titled "VERY Disappointed" is one of the most-read critical accounts of Lindblad currently online. It concerns a Svalbard sailing. The reviewer praised the ship, crew and expedition staff, and reported one core disappointment: two polar-bear sightings, five bears total, at a distance the reviewer says even a long zoom lens struggled to resolve — against an expectation of closer encounters. A secondary complaint describes a mid-voyage illness-isolation incident and a forfeited deposit on a subsequently cancelled future booking.

What Lindblad's current itinerary page says

Lindblad's current Svalbard itinerary page uses deliberately hedged language about sightings — "multiple opportunities to experience sightings" — with no numeric or proximity guarantee. Whether that wording was different at the time of this reviewer's trip could not be verified.

FAQ

Does Lindblad guarantee polar bear sightings in Svalbard?

No. Lindblad's current Svalbard itinerary page uses hedged language about sighting opportunities with no numeric or proximity guarantee, and at least one detailed review describes a trip with only distant sightings.

Is the "VERY Disappointed" review representative of most Lindblad Arctic sailings?

This site cannot say with certainty either way — wildlife sightings in a wild, unscripted environment vary voyage to voyage by nature. The review is one detailed, verifiable data point, not a confirmed pattern across every Arctic departure.
