The fleet

Scenic Eclipse I and Scenic Eclipse II each carry 228 berths but are capped at 200 in Antarctic waters, Polar Class 6. Both carry helicopters and a submersible — hardware shared with only one other operator in this ranking, Silversea, at a smaller scale. At 200 in Antarctica, landings run as group rotation in shifts of up to 100.

FAQ

Does Scenic Eclipse actually use its helicopters and submersible in Antarctica?

Reviewers note the hardware is present and impressive, but some report the shore-excursion programme doesn't always make full use of it — ask an agent how frequently these are deployed on the specific itinerary you're booking.

How does Scenic's price compare to the rest of this ranking?

At $20,000 and up, Scenic has the second-highest entry price in this ranking, offset by an editorial price/value score of 45 that reflects extensive inclusions — drinks, gratuities and butler service.

What do Scenic Antarctica cruise reviews complain about most?

A gap between the marketed premium positioning and delivered service responsiveness on some voyages, plus isolated operational issues such as charter-flight delays.
