Contact

Three routes, depending on why you're writing. Email editor@lindblad-expeditions-reviews.com with one of the subjects below.

Corrections

Subject: Correction. Include the page, the specific figure, and a source. Verified corrections are applied to the live data and reflected in the next ranking recalculation.

Data updates

Subject: Data update. For operators or ships already listed — new pricing, a capacity change, an updated ice-class rating.

Operator submissions

Subject: Submit operator. See the full inclusion criteria on the Submit an Operator page before writing.

This site aims to respond within a reasonable window and does not promise a specific turnaround it can't guarantee. This is a static site with no server-side form processing, which is why every route above is a direct email address rather than a contact form.
