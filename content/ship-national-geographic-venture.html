National Geographic Venture Review 2026

A 2018-built coastal ship working Alaska and Baja California, National Geographic Venture is praised for Zodiac-friendly design and criticised, like much of the fleet, on cost relative to value. For the fleet-wide picture, see the Lindblad Expeditions review.

At a glance

Guests
100 (50 cabins)
Ice class
None — coastal ship, not ice-strengthened for polar sailing
Built
2018, Nichols Brothers Boat Builders, Washington State
Regions
Alaska, British Columbia, Baja California, Sea of Cortés

Equipment

A Zodiac fleet and paddleboards are standard aboard. Venture's boarding platforms are purpose-built for coastal Zodiac operations, a design reviewers call an improvement over older expedition-ship layouts.

FAQ

Where does National Geographic Venture sail?

Alaska and British Columbia in one season, Baja California and the Sea of Cortés in another — both coastal, non-polar itineraries.

Is National Geographic Venture ice-classed?

No. She is a coastal ship, not built or classed for polar ice.
