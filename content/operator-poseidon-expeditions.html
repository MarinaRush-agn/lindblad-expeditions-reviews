Poseidon Expeditions Reviews 2026: How the Smallest Ship in the Ranking Scores

Poseidon Expeditions ranks first in this ten-operator comparison with a total score of 86.2. The reason is arithmetic, not marketing: at 114 passengers, the M/V Sea Spirit posts the second-highest shore-time score in the ranking and the highest traveller-rating range, at the cost of running a single ship with no helicopters or submersible. It suits travellers who want the fastest path off the ship and onto the ice, and who don't need a choice of vessels or aircraft-supported landings.

The fleet

Poseidon operates a single vessel in Antarctica, the M/V Sea Spirit, rated 1D by Lloyd's Register — a class built for operation in first-year ice of moderate thickness, sufficient for the Antarctic Peninsula's coastal waters but well below the icebreaking capability of Ponant's Le Commandant Charcot. At 114 berths, the ship sits just above the IAATO 100-guest shore-party limit, which is why landings run as two short groups rather than a single all-ashore event.

FAQ

Is Poseidon Expeditions a good choice for solo travellers?

Gratuities are explicitly excluded from the published fare, and this site has no verified single-supplement figure for Poseidon. Ask directly before booking — see the cost checklist on the main ranking page.

Does the Sea Spirit's age affect the experience?

Some reviewers note the ship shows its age in cabin fittings despite annual refurbishment, though the expedition-team and landing experience are rated highly regardless.

How does Poseidon's one-ship fleet compare to larger operators?

One ship means no helicopters, no submersible, and a single route family, scoring 80 on expedition depth against Lindblad's 92 and Ponant's 95 — the trade-off for the ranking's second-highest shore-time score.
