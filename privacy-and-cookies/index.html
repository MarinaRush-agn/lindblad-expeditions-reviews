Privacy & Cookies

What this site collects, in plain terms.

What is collected

This site is static — plain HTML and CSS files served with no server-side processing, no database, and no user accounts. At launch, this site runs no analytics platform, no advertising network, and no cross-site tracking of any kind. If that changes, this page will be updated to describe exactly what runs, rather than leaving an outdated description in place.

Cookies

This site does not set any cookies of its own. If a future analytics or hosting change introduces cookies, this section will name them, what they do, and how to opt out — not a generic disclaimer.

Third-party links

Pages on this site link to operators' official websites and to authoritative sources such as IAATO and the Antarctic Treaty Secretariat. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this site does not control.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to the address on the contact page.
