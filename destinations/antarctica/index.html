Lindblad Expeditions in Antarctica: Reviews and What to Expect

Three ships sail Lindblad's Antarctic season, and the review pattern is consistent across all of them: naturalist quality and ship handling praised, food-for-price and pre-departure administration the recurring complaints. What reviewers say about Lindblad in Antarctica

Based on this site's reading of the Cruise Critic sample across all three Antarctic ships

Expedition team

Naturalist depth is the single most consistent praise across the sample — reviewers repeatedly single out specific staff by name for wildlife and glaciology knowledge.

Cruise Critic
Ship handling

Resolution and Endurance's X-Bow hulls are credited with a smoother Drake Passage crossing than older polar ships; Explorer's smaller scale gets praised for open-bridge wildlife access.

Cruise Critic
Food for the price

At a $10,000-plus starting fare, several reviewers expected a stronger dining programme than what was delivered, particularly on Explorer.

Cruise Critic
Pre-departure administration

The most consistent criticism in the entire Lindblad review sample concentrates here — booking handling, flight-class misrepresentation, marketing promises that didn't match delivery — not the Antarctic voyage itself.

Cruise Critic

FAQ

Which Lindblad ships sail to Antarctica?

National Geographic Resolution (138 guests), National Geographic Endurance (126 guests), and National Geographic Explorer (148 guests) — all three run the Antarctic season, late October through March.

How does Lindblad's Antarctic fleet compare to other operators?

Lindblad ranks fourth of ten in this site's Antarctic comparison, with an effective capacity of 137.3 across its three ships — larger than Poseidon's single 114-guest ship, comparable to Aurora and Oceanwide.
