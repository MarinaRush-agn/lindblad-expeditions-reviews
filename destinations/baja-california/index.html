Lindblad Expeditions in Baja California: Reviews and What to Expect

Lindblad's winter Baja California and Sea of Cortés season is built around whale encounters, and the review pattern reflects that directly — praised when the whales show up in volume, criticised when the time on the water falls short of what the itinerary implied. This page covers Baja specifically; for Lindblad's overall rating see the Lindblad Expeditions review.

What reviewers say about Lindblad in Baja California

Based on this site's reading of the Cruise Critic sample for Lindblad's Baja California sailings

Whale encounters

Multi-species whale sightings — blue, gray and humpback across different parts of the season — are the most consistently praised element of this destination in the review sample.

Time on the water

Some reviewers report less actual whale-watching time on the water than the itinerary description implied, a destination-specific version of the expectation-versus-delivery pattern also seen in the Arctic.

FAQ

What whales can you see on a Lindblad Baja California trip?

Reviewers report blue, gray and humpback whale sightings across different parts of the November–April season, in addition to other marine wildlife in the Sea of Cortés.

Is Baja California good for families?

Yes — National Geographic Explorers-in-Training runs on every Baja California departure, with a minimum age of six on most family sailings.
